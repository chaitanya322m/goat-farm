Buying Goats

Before purchasing a goat, check its overall condition and farm records.

01

Check the Goat

Observe activity, body condition, eyes, coat and general appearance.

02

Ask About History

Ask the seller about age, feeding and previous care.

03

Check Records

Ask about vaccination and veterinary records where available.

04

Get Expert Advice

For an important purchase, consult a qualified veterinarian.

🌱

Feed & Nutrition

Good nutrition supports healthy growth and farm productivity.

🌿 Green Fodder

Suitable green fodder can be included according to the goats' nutritional needs.

🌾 Dry Fodder

Good-quality dry fodder can form part of the daily diet.

💧 Clean Water

Fresh, clean drinking water should always be available.

🧂 Minerals

Mineral requirements depend on age, production and diet.

❤️

Goat Health Care

Regular observation and professional veterinary care are important for a healthy herd.

👀 Daily Observation

Observe eating, drinking, movement and general behavior.

💉 Vaccination

Follow a vaccination schedule recommended by a veterinarian.

🩺 Veterinary Care

Contact a qualified veterinarian when a goat appears unwell.

🧹 Clean Farm

Keep housing clean and dry to support good farm hygiene.

🏠

Farm & Shed

A suitable goat shed should provide protection, cleanliness and good airflow.

🏠 Shed

Provide enough space for the animals and easy farm management.

🌬️ Ventilation

Good airflow helps maintain a comfortable environment.

🧹 Cleanliness

Regular cleaning helps maintain good hygiene in the shed.

☀️ Dry Environment

Keep resting areas reasonably dry and protected from weather.
